Three tiers, one look

Everyone gets the basket. The basket knows who you are.

Same cane basket, same ivory muslin, same kumkum ribbon and monogram tag for every room, so no guest can tell the tiers apart at a glance. What changes is what is inside.

What goes in

The contents matrix.

Diamonds mark what a tier gets. Gold diamonds are upgrades of the same item (a bigger box, a better tea).

Packaging

How it looks on the bed.

BasketRound cane basket with handle, 30 cm (tier 3) or oval 38 cm (tiers 1–2). Natural finish, no varnish.
LiningIvory mulmul (muslin) square, 60 cm, gathered up and over the contents. Doubles as a dupatta-sized cloth guests keep.
RibbonKumkum satin, 25 mm, single knot (no bow). Tag threaded through the knot.
Tag90 × 55 mm, kumkum card with gold foil monogram, the guest’s name handwritten in gold ink. Printable below.
NoteA6 ivory card, letterpress if budget allows, else 300 gsm uncoated. Printable below. Sits on top, name facing up.
PlacementCentred on the bed, magazine leaning against it, water on the nightstand. Housekeeping gets a photo of the “correct” bed.
