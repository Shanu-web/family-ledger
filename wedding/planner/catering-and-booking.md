## Accommodation and access

All 55 rooms, all lawns, the banquet hall, dining hall and kitchen are
reserved exclusively for the wedding, 10–12 December 2026. Housekeeping and
room service throughout. Check-in from the morning of 10 Dec and check-out on
12 Dec are flexible to arrivals and departures. The guest list goes to the
resort before arrival; a designated reception staff member allocates rooms and
keys against it, coordinates welcome drinks and helps with check-in/out.
